# User & Administrator Operational Guide

## For Users

### 1. Account Setup
1. Visit the registration page at `/register` and enter your full name, email, phone, city, and secure password.
2. After logging in, you will be redirected to your personal **Safety Dashboard**.

### 2. Configuring Emergency Contacts
1. Navigate to **Contacts** (`/contacts`) and click **+ Add New Contact**.
2. Enter the person's name, relationship, and phone number.
3. Toggle **Mark as Trusted Contact** for your primary emergency responder.
4. Trusted contacts will appear at the top of your **Active SOS** screen with one-touch call buttons.

### 3. Elevating Your Safety Readiness Score
1. Open the **Safety Checklist** (`/checklist`) and execute the listed precautions (e.g. saving emergency numbers, checking location privacy).
2. Check off items as you finish them. Your score will update dynamically.
3. Browse the **Safety Tips** library (`/tips`) and read guides to earn preparedness credits.

### 4. Activating SOS Assistance
1. When you feel in danger, click the **🚨 SOS** button in the header or visit `/sos`.
2. Confirm the activation prompt. Your browser coordinates and optional landmark notes will be securely recorded.
3. The platform transitions into **Active Emergency Command Station** mode, providing direct dials to your contacts, guidance checklist, and verified nearby police/medical centers.
4. When you are safe, click **Mark Safe & Resolve** and enter resolution notes.

---

## For Administrators

### 1. Accessing Admin Center
Log in with an administrator account (e.g. `admin@womensafety.org`) and select **Admin Center** (`/admin/dashboard`).

### 2. Managing Active SOS Alerts
1. Open **SOS Events** (`/admin/sos`).
2. Active alerts are highlighted in coral.
3. You can click into an active event, coordinate with emergency liaisons, or resolve/cancel alerts on behalf of users.

### 3. Reviewing Incident Reports
1. Open **Incidents** (`/admin/incidents`).
2. Click **Review & Action** on any report.
3. Update the investigation status (*UNDER REVIEW, RESOLVED, CLOSED*) and provide resolution notes. The reporting user will be automatically notified.

### 4. Generating Operational Reports & Analytics
1. View real-time visual charts under **Analytics** (`/analytics`).
2. Under **Reports** (`/reports`), select a dataset and filter by date/severity/status.
3. Click **Export to CSV**, **Export to JSON**, or **Print Report** for audit compliance.
